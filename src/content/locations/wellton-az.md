Wellton is a small farming town along I-8, roughly 30 miles east of Yuma, sitting within the Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation District — one of the more extensive agricultural canal and drainage networks in the service area. That geography makes canal- and lateral-adjacent barrier wall questions come up here more often than in most of the surrounding towns.

## Canal-adjacent barrier walls are common here

A meaningfully higher share of Wellton-area properties border an irrigation canal or drainage lateral than in Yuma's more urban neighborhoods, given the town's location inside the Wellton-Mohawk Irrigation District. City of Yuma-area code sets a 6 ft minimum height and a solid-masonry-minimum requirement (with wrought iron allowed as a specific alternate) for barrier walls adjacent to canals or drainage channels — genuinely relevant, practical content for a large share of Wellton property owners rather than a rare edge case. Confirm the current spec with the relevant irrigation district and local zoning authority before finalizing any wall plan near canal frontage.

## A separate, smaller zoning authority

Wellton is its own small incorporated town, and zoning/building requirements should be confirmed directly with the Town of Wellton (or Yuma County, for properties outside town limits) rather than assumed from City of Yuma code, even though the same broad canal-barrier-wall logic tends to apply given the shared irrigation-district geography.
