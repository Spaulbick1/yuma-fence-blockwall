San Luis is one of Yuma County's fastest-growing incorporated cities, sitting on the U.S.–Mexico border directly across from San Luis Río Colorado, Sonora. Its population has grown substantially in recent years, and that growth shows up in fencing and wall demand two ways: new-construction perimeter fencing on freshly built lots, and replacement/repair work on older properties as the city's housing stock ages.

## A separate municipal code from the city of Yuma

San Luis is its own incorporated city with its own zoning and building code — a genuinely important distinction from the unincorporated county areas and from the city of Yuma itself, whose specific fencing/wall ordinance (Municipal Code § 154-15.06) doesn't automatically apply here. Anyone planning a fence or wall project in San Luis should confirm current height, setback, and permit requirements directly with the City of San Luis rather than assuming Yuma's rules carry over.

## Agricultural and border-adjacent context

San Luis sits within Yuma County's broader agricultural economy, and many properties in and around the city border farmland or irrigation infrastructure — a relevant factor for anyone whose fence or wall project involves a canal-, lateral-, or field-adjacent property line, where drainage and access easements can affect what's allowed.
