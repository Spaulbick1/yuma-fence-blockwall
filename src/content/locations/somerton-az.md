Somerton is a historic agricultural community in Yuma County's Colorado River farming region, southwest of the city of Yuma. Its economy and much of its surrounding land remain tied to agriculture, which shapes fencing and wall demand differently than in Yuma's more urban neighborhoods — properties here more often border farmland, irrigation canals, or agricultural easements rather than another residential lot.

## A separate municipal code from the city of Yuma

Like San Luis, Somerton is its own incorporated city with its own zoning and building code, distinct from the City of Yuma's specific § 154-15.06 fencing/wall ordinance. Anyone planning a project here should confirm current requirements directly with the City of Somerton rather than assuming Yuma's rules apply.

## Agricultural-adjacent fencing and wall considerations

For properties bordering farmland, a canal, or an irrigation lateral, the fence or wall line often needs to account for drainage flow, access easements for irrigation maintenance, and — depending on exact location — the same kind of barrier-wall height and material considerations that apply to canal-adjacent lots elsewhere in the Yuma area. Flag this context to any contractor quoting the job so the design accounts for it from the start rather than after work begins.
